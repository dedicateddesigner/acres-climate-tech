# ACRES — Project Brief

## Project

ACRES is a climate-tech company focused on turning carbon removal into value for Indian agriculture.

## Primary positioning

Turning Carbon Into a New Source of Value for Indian Agriculture.

## Core proposition

ACRES helps industries meet climate commitments and farmers build resilient, income-generating soil through Distributed Biochar and Enhanced Rock Weathering.

## Primary audiences

- Industry / climate-conscious organizations
- Farming communities
- Partners
- Investors

## Primary conversion

Encourage qualified visitors to contact ACRES and explore partnership opportunities.

## Website objective

The website should communicate:

1. What ACRES does
2. Why the problem matters
3. How Biochar works
4. How Enhanced Rock Weathering works
5. How ACRES operates
6. Evidence of real-world projects
7. Where materials are sourced
8. How visitors can engage with ACRES

## Design principle

The website should feel credible, scientific, grounded, premium and connected to agriculture.

It must avoid generic "green technology" visual language.

## Important rule

Do not invent company facts, statistics, project data, sourcing locations, claims, certifications or impact numbers.

Anything marked as placeholder in the content brief must remain clearly identifiable until confirmed.
